Numerals — one number, eleven ways to write it

Explore how numbers are written across scripts, cultures, and centuries. Type a number and Numerals writes it in Devanagari, Gujarati, Punjabi, Tamil, Eastern Arabic or Persian digits, or in Roman, Japanese, Greek, Hebrew or Egyptian hieroglyphic notation.

  • Compare at a glance. See the same value in another script or tradition as you type.
  • Choose the notation. Additive Roman fours, Japanese financial characters, Greek stigma, Hebrew marks.
  • Take it with you. Long press the result to copy it as text or share it.
