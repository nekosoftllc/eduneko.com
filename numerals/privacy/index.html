Privacy Policy for Eduneko Numerals

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

1. Overview

Eduneko Numerals (“the App”) is developed by Eduneko OÜ. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Eduneko Numerals does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage information.

  • No Accounts: You are not required to create an account or provide any personal details to use the App.
  • No Network Requests: All numeral conversion logic and computations are processed locally on your device.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The App does not contain any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or tracking tools.
  • Settings Stay on Your Device: The App saves only your chosen numeral system and its display options on your device, so they are ready next time. The numbers you enter are held in memory while you use the App and are not saved or transmitted.

3. App Store & Apple Services

Because you download the App through Apple’s App Store, Apple may collect anonymized installation and crash report data according to your device’s global privacy settings. We do not aggregate or link this data to individual users.

4. Third-Party Links

If the App or its description links to external web pages (such as eduneko.com), visiting those pages is subject to the privacy policy of that specific website.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
